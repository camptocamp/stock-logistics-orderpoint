**General Settings > Inventory**

- **Safety Stock History Days**: Define the rolling window for the historical analysis to compute the safety stock. Default: 365. Company-specific.

**Orderpoint (per reordering rule)**

- **Safety stock method**: `Manual` (min/max editable) or `Cycle Service Level` (computed).
- **Cycle Service Level**: Defines target probability and thus z-score. (e.g: 90%, 95%, 97%, 99%)
- **Growth factor** (optional): multiplier on safety stock to account for expected growth in demand.
